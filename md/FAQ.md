# FAQ

Q: Is real money used in this app?
A: No — this is a paper trading simulator using virtual balances only.

Q: Are prices real-time?
A: Depends on the connected market API; some providers offer delayed data
   on free tiers.

Q: Can I withdraw virtual cash?
A: No — virtual cash is only for simulation and learning.

Q: Is my data private?
A: See `md/PRIVACY.md` for details on what we collect and how it is used.
