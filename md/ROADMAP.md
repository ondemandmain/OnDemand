# Roadmap

This roadmap gives a short view of planned milestones and priorities.

MVP (Phase 1)

- User accounts and initial virtual balance (₹1,00,000 default)
- Market data integration (single provider)
- Buy/sell simulation and portfolio calculations
- Basic leaderboard and private leagues

Phase 2 — Social & polish

- Friend invites and league management UI
- Improved charts and trade history
- Real-time price updates and notifications

Phase 3 — Scale & integrations

- Multiple market data providers and redundancy
- Mobile app or responsive PWA
- Analytics, metrics, and export of historical data

Longer-term ideas

- Strategy backtesting sandbox
- Teaching modules and guided challenges
- Premium features for advanced data feeds

How to help

- Prioritize issues tagged `help wanted` or `good first issue`.
- Suggest changes by opening issues in the repo.
